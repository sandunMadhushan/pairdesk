PairDesk 0.13.0 brings **direct connections**: away from home, the screen now goes straight from your computer to your phone whenever the networks allow it, instead of through the relay. Update both the computer and the phone (and the relay, if you run your own).

### New in 0.13.0

- **Direct connections.** When the phone can't reach the computer on the same Wi-Fi, the PairDesk relay introduces them and they try to open a direct path through both routers. When it works, the relay is out of the way: lower delay, and nothing passes through it. Where the networks don't allow it (many mobile carriers and offices), the relay carries the connection as before. The phone's title bar says **Direct** or **Relay**, and **Remote access** on the computer shows how the last phone connected. Same encryption as always: the certificate is pinned and the phone signs in on every route.
- **Router port, automatically.** The computer asks your router to forward a port to it (UPnP or NAT-PMP). When the router agrees and you have a public address, the phone connects with no relay at all. Turn it off under *Remote access → Open a port on the router automatically*.
- **IPv6.** The computer also listens on IPv6 and tells the phone its IPv6 addresses.
- **Relay: STUN.** The relay answers STUN over UDP on its ports, which direct connections need. If you run your own relay, open **UDP 443 and 47900** too (see `docs/RELAY.md`).

### Good to know

- A direct path shows each device the other's IP address, as any direct connection does. Turn *Direct connections* off on the computer to always use the relay.
- An older phone or computer keeps working through the relay.

### Before you install

- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
