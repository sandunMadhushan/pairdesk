PairDesk 0.16.0 adds presenter mode to the phone app.

### New in 0.16.0

- **Present** (Android app): open it from the *Present* card on the Shortcuts tab, or the slideshow button at the top of the control screen. It has large **Next** and **Back** buttons, and the phone's **volume keys** also turn slides while it is open (the screen stays on). A **talk timer** counts up, or down to a length you choose (5 to 60 minutes) and turns red when you run over. **Start** (F5) and **From here** (Shift+F5) begin the slide show, **Blank** (B) blacks out the screen, **End show** is Escape.
- **Laser:** switches on PowerPoint's laser pointer (Ctrl+L) and shows a small pad on the phone that moves the pointer. In other programs it is an ordinary pointer. A laser dot that works in every program is not built yet.
- It works with PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress, PDF viewers and browsers' full-screen slides, because it sends the same keys they all use. No computer-side update is needed; PairDesk 0.16.0 on the computer is the same as 0.15.3 for this.

### Before you install

- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
