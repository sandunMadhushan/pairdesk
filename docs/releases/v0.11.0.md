PairDesk 0.11.0 adds pairing links, a way to help someone else with their computer, and a relay so PairDesk works away from home without Tailscale. It includes everything from 0.10.1 and 0.10.0. For 0.9 and earlier, see the [0.10.1 notes](https://github.com/sandunMadhushan/pairdesk/releases/tag/v0.10.1).

### New in 0.11.0

- **Pair with a link.** Under the QR code, PairDesk on the computer now shows a link with a **Copy** button. Send it to your phone (a chat with yourself, an email) and tap it there: PairDesk opens and pairs. It works once, for 10 minutes. In the app, **Paste a link** (top bar, or on the first screen) works too. Links go from the computer to the phone: the phone is the one that connects, so the computer has to hand out its address and a one-time code.
- **Get help.** Someone who needs help opens PairDesk on their computer, presses **Get help** on Home and sends you the link. You tap it, confirm, and can see and control their screen. Your access is limited and temporary:
  - no power or session actions, no files and no sound, whatever their settings say;
  - it ends after 2 hours, 5 minutes after you disconnect, or as soon as they press **End help** (their PairDesk shows a banner while you're connected);
  - your phone then forgets that computer by itself.
- **Works away from home without Tailscale.** When the phone can't reach the computer directly, it now connects through the PairDesk relay. The connection stays encrypted end to end between your phone and your computer, with the same certificate check as on Wi-Fi: the relay only passes data along and can't see your screen, what you type, files or sound. Direct connections (Wi-Fi, Tailscale) are still tried first. On the computer, **Remote access → PairDesk relay** shows whether it's connected; you can turn it off or use your own relay ([RELAY.md](https://github.com/sandunMadhushan/pairdesk/blob/main/docs/RELAY.md)). Phones learn the relay route the next time they connect at home, or from a new pairing link.

### From 0.10.1

- **Talk and listen at the same time.** In 0.10.0, the phone muted the computer while you talked, so with *Keep talking* on you couldn't hear the computer's microphone. Now both directions stay open, and echo cancelling on both sides stops you hearing your own voice come back: WebRTC's echo canceller on the computer (only while you talk; music is untouched otherwise), and Android's call mode on the phone. While you talk, the phone plays through its loudspeaker, or your headphones if they're connected. Headphones give the best sound.

### From 0.10.0: sound

Tap the **headphones** button at the top of the phone app:

- **Computer sound.** Hear what the computer plays (videos, music, calls) on your phone.
- **Talk to the computer.** Hold the big microphone button (or turn on *Keep talking*) and your voice comes out of the computer's speakers. The phone asks once for microphone access.
- **Computer's microphone.** Hear people near the computer. This is **off** until you allow it on the computer (PairDesk → Permissions → *Hear the microphone*). While a phone listens, the tray icon shows a red dot and the PairDesk window says so, with a Stop button.

Sound uses Opus (about 96 kbit/s while something plays, almost nothing in silence), so it works well over Tailscale too. It stops while the phone app is in the background. Both kinds of sound can be switched off on the computer (Permissions → *Sound and talking*).

### From 0.10.0: sound

Tap the **headphones** button at the top of the phone app to hear the computer, talk through its speakers (hold the microphone button, or turn on *Keep talking*), or hear its microphone (off until allowed on the computer under Permissions → *Hear the microphone*).

### Before you install

- **Phone:** if you have 0.7 or older, uninstall it first. Earlier test builds used a temporary signing key, so Android won't install over them. Then install this version and scan the pairing code again; remove the old phone on the computer's PairDesk page. This happens only once.
- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- A PairDesk already running on the computer is stopped and replaced by the setup.
