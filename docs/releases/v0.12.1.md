PairDesk 0.12.1 gives the computer app the same new look as the phone, and makes its window feel like a normal desktop program. Update the computer; the phone app is unchanged.

### New in 0.12.1

- **A new design for the computer window.** Soft grey and white with one lime accent, and a dark mode that follows Windows. A sidebar with Home, Phones, Files, Shortcuts and your settings; Home shows the connection at a glance.
- **Dialogs inside the window.** Pairing a phone, Get help, new shortcuts and "Are you sure?" questions open over the window, not as browser popups. Messages appear as a small bar at the bottom.
- **It behaves like an app.** No browser right-click menu, reload, zoom or hand cursors. On Windows 11 the title bar takes the page's colour.
- **A new icon** in black and lime for the program, taskbar, tray, installer and the Linux packages.
- **Linux:** the window opens without tabs or an address bar when Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave is installed.

### Before you install

- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
