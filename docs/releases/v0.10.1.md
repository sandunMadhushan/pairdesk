PairDesk 0.10.1 makes talking work like a call: you and the person at the computer can talk and hear each other at the same time. It includes everything from 0.10.0 and earlier, which weren't published separately.

### New in 0.10.1

- **Talk and listen at the same time.** In 0.10.0, the phone muted the computer while you talked, so with *Keep talking* on you couldn't hear the computer's microphone. Now both directions stay open, and echo cancelling on both sides stops you hearing your own voice come back: WebRTC's echo canceller on the computer (only while you talk; music is untouched otherwise), and Android's call mode on the phone. While you talk, the phone plays through its loudspeaker, or your headphones if they're connected. Headphones give the best sound.

### From 0.10.0: sound

Tap the **headphones** button at the top of the phone app:

- **Computer sound.** Hear what the computer plays (videos, music, calls) on your phone.
- **Talk to the computer.** Hold the big microphone button (or turn on *Keep talking*) and your voice comes out of the computer's speakers. The phone asks once for microphone access.
- **Computer's microphone.** Hear people near the computer. This is **off** until you allow it on the computer (PairDesk → Permissions → *Hear the microphone*). While a phone listens, the tray icon shows a red dot and the PairDesk window says so, with a Stop button.

Sound uses Opus (about 96 kbit/s while something plays, almost nothing in silence), so it works well over Tailscale too. It stops while the phone app is in the background. Both kinds of sound can be switched off on the computer (Permissions → *Sound and talking*).

### From 0.9.1

- **Fixed: the phone app closed when opening the Screen tab** with *Smooth screen view* on. If the phone's video decoder ever fails again, the app now switches to pictures by itself instead of closing, and if it should still close during video, the next start uses pictures (turn *Settings → Smooth screen view* back on to try again). Smooth video now needs Android 10 or newer; older phones get pictures.
- **PairDesk looks like a real app now.** Instead of one long page, it has a sidebar with separate screens: Home, Phones, Files, Shortcuts, Permissions, Remote access, Sign-in screen (Windows) and Settings. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 switch between them.
- **Windows: PairDesk has its own window.** Opening PairDesk (Start menu, tray icon, or starting it again) shows the PairDesk window instead of a browser tab. It uses WebView2, which is part of Windows 10 and 11. Links to websites still open in your browser, and the tray menu has *Open in the browser* if you prefer that. On a PC without WebView2, the browser is used as before.

### From 0.9.0

- **Smoother screen view.** The computer now sends real video (H.264) instead of a stream of pictures. Scrolling, dragging windows and playing videos look much smoother, and it uses less data. Phones that can't show the video get pictures as before, automatically. If the screen view misbehaves, turn off *Settings → Smooth screen view* in the phone app.
- **Windows: lock screen, sign-in screen and administrator prompts.** Windows shows these on a protected screen that normal programs can't control, so until now the phone couldn't use them. On the computer, open PairDesk and turn on **Sign-in screen and admin prompts**; Windows asks for administrator permission once. After that, your phone can:
  - unlock the computer (type your PIN or password),
  - answer administrator (UAC) prompts,
  - use the sign-in screen after a restart, before anyone signs in.

  It works through a small helper service. Turn it off on the same page, or by uninstalling PairDesk.

### From 0.8.0

- **Windows setup.** Installs without administrator rights, with a Start menu entry and an entry in Settings → Apps.
- **Linux packages.** `.deb`, `.rpm` and AppImage.
- **Automatic updates.** The computer checks once a day and offers *Install update*. Updates are signed and checked before anything is installed. The phone app (GitHub copy) tells you when a new version is out.
- **New icon**, website and privacy policy at [pairdesk.madhushan.me](https://pairdesk.madhushan.me).

### Before you install

- **Phone:** if you have 0.7 or older, uninstall it first. Earlier test builds used a temporary signing key, so Android won't install over them. Then install this version and scan the pairing code again; remove the old phone on the computer's PairDesk page. This happens only once.
- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- A PairDesk already running on the computer is stopped and replaced by the setup.
