PairDesk 0.14.0 makes it easier to get the phone app, to update it, and to support the project.

### New in 0.14.0

- **Get the phone app from your computer.** The computer app has a *Get the phone app* card with a QR code (Settings), a button on the home screen, and a link in the pairing window. They all lead to <https://pairdesk.madhushan.me/app/>, which sends Android phones to the download today and will send phones to Google Play and the App Store once the app is there.
- **Update from Settings (phone).** *Check for updates* now shows the update with an **Update** button right there; you no longer have to go back to the Home screen. The Home screen still shows it too, and PairDesk checks again (at most once a day) whenever you open it.
- **Support PairDesk.** A *Support PairDesk* card in the computer app and in the phone's Settings → About (GitHub, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee), and the *Sponsor* button on the GitHub page. Left out of the Microsoft Store version, the F-Droid build, and later the Google Play and App Store builds, because those stores have their own rules for donations.

### Before you install

- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
