PairDesk 0.12.0 gives the phone app a new look and a clearer layout. Update the phone; the computer is unchanged and every feature works as before.

### New in 0.12.0

- **A new design.** Soft grey and white with one lime accent, and a matching dark mode that follows your phone's setting. The app has a new font and icon, and a splash screen when it opens.
- **A bottom bar for everything you do while connected.** Touchpad, Screen, Shortcuts, Files and Sound are always one tap away, so sharing files and hearing the computer are easy to find. Power is at the top right.
- **Type from the Screen view.** A Type button opens the keyboard on Screen as well as on Touchpad, including full screen.
- **Computers, Add and Settings** are the home bar; Add opens scan or paste a link.
- **Motion with a purpose.** Tabs slide, sheets rise, buttons press in, and sound and talking show live activity. With Android's "Remove animations" on, everything simply fades.

### Before you install

- The phone app installs over the old one when both come from the official releases.
- **Windows:** the setup isn't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*. Check downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
