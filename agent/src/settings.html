Pair a phone

Pair once, then your phone connects whenever this computer is on.

  1. Open PairDesk on your phone.
  2. Tap Scan pairing code and point the camera at this code.

Phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. The code works once and expires in .

Control on this desktop

This desktop (Wayland) asks before an app may move the pointer, type or see the screen. Choose Allow control, then in the window that opens tick Allow Remote Interaction (keep Allow Clipboard Access on), pick your screen, keep Remember This Selection on and press Share. While a phone is connected, your desktop shows that it's being shared.

    Connect from anywhere

    Checking for Tailscale…
    1. Install Tailscale on this computer and sign in. It's free for personal use.
    2. Install Tailscale on your phone (Google Play or F-Droid) and sign in with the same account.
    3. Open PairDesk on your phone once while you're on the same Wi-Fi, so it learns this computer's Tailscale address.

        Sign-in screen and admin prompts

        Windows shows the lock screen, the sign-in screen and administrator prompts (UAC) on a protected screen that normal programs can't see or control. Turn this on to use them from your phone too, including at the sign-in screen after a restart.

        Windows asks for administrator permission. PairDesk then installs a small helper service ("PairDesk helper") and keeps a copy of your pairing where only Windows itself can read it, so phones you paired can reach the sign-in screen.

        Good to know: like any remote control that can answer administrator prompts, this also means a program already running on your account could answer one. Turn it on if you need it, and keep your phone's app lock on.
