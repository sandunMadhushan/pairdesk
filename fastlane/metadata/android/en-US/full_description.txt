<p>PairDesk turns your phone into a remote for your Windows or Linux computer. Pair once by scanning a code; after that the app connects by itself whenever you open it.</p>

<p><b>What you can do</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Use the phone as a touchpad and keyboard: move, click, scroll, drag and type</li>
<li>See the computer's screen and control it by touch, like a touchpad</li>
<li>Key combinations, media and volume keys, and your own shortcut buttons</li>
<li>Open apps and shortcuts, lock, sleep, restart or shut down</li>
<li>Copy and paste between phone and computer</li>
<li>Send files both ways, including from other apps with Share → PairDesk</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Private by design</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Your phone talks to your computer directly. There is no PairDesk server and no account.</li>
<li>Connections are encrypted, and only phones you paired can connect.</li>
<li>You decide on the computer what paired phones may do.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Getting started</b></p>
<ol>
<li>Install PairDesk on your computer from <a href="https://pairdesk.madhushan.me">pairdesk.madhushan.me</a>.</li>
<li>It shows a pairing code. Open this app and scan it.</li>
<li>That's it. The phone connects on the same Wi-Fi, or from anywhere with Tailscale.</li>
</ol>

<p>PairDesk is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later).</p>
